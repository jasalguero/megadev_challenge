The three problems

parser: the shared parser reads day-first dates month first. This is the bug #4471 describes. It hits every day-first tenant, through the app, email and the booking-v2 form. Fixed by fix(dates): parse dd/mm for eu locales. The desk's own view counts 379 here, because it files the 5 one-day reports under parser by tenant.

hint: the booking-v3 form tells day-first people to type month first. The form prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field. People type what the hint says, and the form reads the date the tenant's way, day first. The parser fix does not touch this path. Only reports sent through that form belong here. Won't fix: the booking-v3 form is retired.

one-day: dates that move by one day, not by a month. In these reports the day and month are not swapped: a booking shows the day before, a client in Lisbon and a client in Toronto get different days for one meeting, an invite arrives before its booking. That points at time zones, not date order. The desk had already read these as a one-day move, then counted them under parser by tenant.
